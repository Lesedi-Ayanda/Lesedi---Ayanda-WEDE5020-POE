About Sisonke Food Bank

Sisonke Food Bank is a community-based organisation dedicated to helping individuals and families who struggle with hunger and food insecurity. Our name, "Sisonke," means "we are together," which represents our belief that we can make a greater difference when we work together as a community.

We collect and distribute food to people in need while encouraging individuals, businesses, schools, and community organisations to get involved in the fight against hunger. We aim to provide more than just food — we provide hope, dignity, and support to those who need it most.

Our goal is to build a caring and united community where no one has to go to bed hungry. Through donations, food drives, community programmes, and partnerships, Sisonke Food Bank is working towards creating a healthier and more secure future for everyone.

Our Mission

Our mission is to fight hunger, support families in need, and build a stronger community by ensuring everyone is well taken care of.

Our Vision

To empower communities by reducing hunger and creating a future filled with hope, dignity, and opportunity.
